# Investment Policy

## Funding routes under consideration

| Partner | Question for diligence | Topics for discussion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Angel or venture fund | Could a paid pilot lead to repeatable licensing or service revenue? Which entity would receive that revenue and hold commercialization rights? | Pilot evidence and budget, followed by potential investment terms, ownership and investor rights. |
| Bank or industrial partner | Could the evidence improve a specific reconciliation or trade-finance workflow? | Pilot scope, operational ownership and acceptance criteria. A credit discussion would also require an identified borrower and repayment source. |
| Grant maker or ecosystem foundation | Could a deliverable benefit other developers? For an Ethereum ecosystem grant, one candidate is an open example for verifying a commitment to a measurement record. | Technical fit, public deliverable, budget and acceptance test under the relevant program. |
| Public innovation agency or regulator | Which public research objective or supervisory question could a trial address? | Funding eligibility under an innovation program or evaluation within a supervisory body's mandate. |

These routes are discussion topics. The funding amount, eligibility and terms depend on the partner and proposed work.
