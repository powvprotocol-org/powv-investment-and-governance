# Investment Policy

PoWV is evaluating how to fund the next stage of development. The appropriate arrangement depends on what the partner would finance and what it would receive. The funding target and financial terms remain open.

| Partner | Proposed arrangement | Basis for a decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Angel investor or venture capital fund | Equity in a company with the rights to commercialize PoWV technology. A potential return would come from profit distributions or a future sale of the stake. | Identity of the company, commercialization rights, use of funds, proposed stake and investor rights. |
| Lender | A loan to an identified borrower, with principal and interest payable on agreed dates. | Borrower cash flow, repayment source, maturity and any proposed collateral. |
| Industrial operator, bank or systems integrator as a customer | A paid pilot addressing a specific operational decision, such as reconciling a delivery or reviewing a treasury authorization. | A named business owner, baseline, price and measurable acceptance criteria. |
| Ecosystem foundation | A grant for a public research or software result. One possible Ethereum project is an open tool that checks whether a record matches a hash previously published onchain. | Usefulness to other developers, program eligibility, budget and a test others can repeat. |
| Public innovation agency | Project funding under a specific research or innovation program. | Fit with the call, applicant eligibility, eligible costs and reporting requirements. |

A regulator could participate in a separate supervisory discussion if the proposed use falls within its mandate. Regulatory assessment carries no assumption of funding or endorsement.
