# Intellectual Property and Licensing

A future structure under consideration would place documented PoWV intellectual property in a holding company and commercial operations in a subsidiary. Adoption depends on legal and tax review and evidence of title for each asset.

| Party | Rights and cash flows under this option |
| --- | --- |
| IP holding company | Would hold the rights it acquires under documented assignments or already owns. A written license would define the technology, permitted uses, territory, term, exclusivity, any royalties and rights to improvements. |
| Operating subsidiary | Would sign pilots and customer contracts, collect customer revenue and pay any agreed royalties. Customer sublicenses would require express authority under the holding company’s license. |
| Investor in the operating subsidiary | Would hold quotas in the subsidiary. Its value would depend in part on continued access to the technology. Material terms for diligence include license renewal and termination, change of control, and who can approve royalty changes or transfers of core IP. |

For each proposed pilot, diligence would trace the relevant rights from the creator or other rights holder to the entity signing the customer contract. Any IP pledged as collateral would require separate documentation.
