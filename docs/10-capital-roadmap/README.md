# Capital Roadmap

## Proposed milestones

1. The project and partner would identify a [workflow](../09-commercial-strategy/README.md) and the decision the pilot would test.
2. The partner and project would repeat the relevant test together. The existing [instrument-to-edge bench record](https://github.com/powvprotocol-org/powv-scale-edge-integration/blob/main/docs/integration-module/experimental-record.md) provides a starting point for physical acquisition. Authorization and financial workflows require tests specific to those uses.
3. Agreed acceptance criteria would guide the pilot budget. The partner would compare results with its baseline before deciding on wider deployment.

The budget, timetable and funding commitments remain open.

Historical research and development inputs are catalogued in the [Technology Capital Formation assessment](../06-financial-model/TECHNOLOGY_CAPITAL_FORMATION.md). This does not constitute a future funding request or an approved capital budget.
