## 1. Purpose and accounting perimeter

This document records how PoWV Protocol is assembling a traceable estimate of the resources and engineering effort associated with its research and development history.

It is designed to support preliminary technical and financial due diligence. The estimate is **not** an audited financial statement, a recognized balance-sheet asset, the company's valuation, an equity contribution, a patent valuation, or a statement that all modeled amounts were paid by a corporate entity.

Three categories are kept separate:

1. **Founder-declared physical acquisitions** — equipment and hardware values that still require invoice, payment and ownership reconciliation.
2. **Modeled recurring infrastructure** — allocation assumptions for electricity, workspace and AI-assisted research tools; not all are verified project-exclusive cash outlays.
3. **Imputed engineering effort** — an estimated replacement-cost proxy for the founder's specialized work, not salary paid, recognized payroll expense or an automatically capitalizable intangible.

## 3. Measurement assumptions

### 3.1 Laboratory and computer equipment

The inventoried physical setup includes a weighing instrument, embedded development boards, serial interfaces, a secure-element component, test instruments, electronic prototyping materials and workspace equipment. Prices are carried as **inventory-line totals**, including multi-unit lines, and therefore are not multiplied by quantity again.

The inventory includes some approximate values. Additional import duties, tax on financial operations (IOF), logistics, installation costs and refundable/non-refundable taxes should be incorporated only after supporting records are checked, avoiding additions that are already embedded in purchase prices.

Hardware ownership and business-use allocation remain to be established independently of possession or experimental use.

### 3.2 Electricity and workspace

The working model currently assigns:

- Electricity: **BRL 260 per month × 16 months = BRL 4,160**.
- Workspace rent: **BRL 700 per month × 16 months = BRL 11,200**.

Both use a full-period **baseline**, not an independently measured project-only proportion. The space also has a residential function. Before either item is classified as a project-attributable cost in formal diligence, the underlying bills and lease/payment records should be reconciled and an allocation methodology (such as floor area, time of use, or dedicated metering) documented. The public baseline must not be interpreted as a conclusion that 100% of the household cost was caused by PoWV development.

This is a **scenario**, not a representation that each subscription was active for all 16 months or that the unidentified USD 20/month was spent.

A final historical schedule will use actual subscription periods, receipts, invoice currencies, charged BRL amounts, tax/IOF treatment and project-use allocations.

### 3.4 Engineering effort and rate

The founder's work is divided into two modeled stages:

| Stage | Period | Modeled hours | Relevant workstreams |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| **A — Research and architecture** | Aug 2025–Mar 2026 | 525 h | Protocol thesis, technical research, architecture, mathematical and governance documentation |
| **B — Engineering and laboratory work** | Apr 2026–20 Nov 2026 | 660 h | Software development, physical-to-edge integration, security research, conformance, testing and technical records |
| **Total** | | **1,185 h** | |

The senior-rate proxy in the model is **BRL 16,250/month ÷ 160 hours/month = BRL 101.5625/hour**, using the 2026 Robert Half median for a Brazilian senior full-stack developer [as attributed by Serasa Experian](https://www.serasaexperian.com.br/carreiras/blog-carreiras/areas-da-tecnologia-mais-bem-pagas/). This is a *working salary-equivalent assumption* for costing skill-replacement effort, not an invoiced consultant rate, independent professional appraisal or independently verified founder timesheet. The **160-hour monthly divisor is a scenario convention**, not a statutory employment divisor. A full sensitivity analysis, relevant payroll-burden references and task-specific benchmarking caveats are in [Benchmarks & IP Valuation](../07-valuation-framework/BENCHMARKS_AND_IP_VALUATION.md).

**Modeled labor value:** 1,185 × BRL 101.5625 = **BRL 120,351.5625**, presented as **BRL 120,351.56**.

Stage durations, activity allocations and actual hours will be reconciled against project records rather than derived automatically from commit counts or the calendar age of the project.

## 6. Recognition and valuation boundaries

**Sourced methodology companion:** [Independent Benchmarks, Burdened Engineering Cost, IPCA, Depreciation & IP Valuation](../07-valuation-framework/BENCHMARKS_AND_IP_VALUATION.md). It addresses employer-cost sensitivity as a separate replacement-cost scenario (not historical paid labor), item-dated inflation adjustment, physical asset useful life, evidence quality, WIPO-recognized IP valuation methods and published World Bank market context. The **BRL 148,275.11** working-model total is unchanged: neither a blanket inflation multiplier nor a speculative IP premium has been silently added.

The purpose of this schedule is **managerial reconstruction and preliminary due diligence**, not automatic capitalization.

- **CPC 27 / IAS 16 — Property, plant and equipment:** requires analysis of acquisition costs, ownership/control, attributable expenditures and subsequent accounting treatment. Acquisition price alone is not the current carrying value.
- **CPC 04 / IAS 38 — Intangible assets:** distinguishes research from development and imposes specific recognition conditions for internally generated intangible assets. Historical conceptual research and unpaid, imputed founder time are not automatically recognized assets.
- **CPC 46 / IFRS 13 — Fair value:** describes fair-value measurement principles. A combined cost scenario is **not** by itself a fair-value assessment or enterprise valuation.
- Financing, taxation, capitalization of costs, intellectual-property title and transfers to any legal entity require their own documentary and professional review.

Official standard references:

- [CPC 04 (R1) — Intangible Assets](https://www.cpc.org.br/CPC/Documentos-Emitidos/Pronunciamentos/Pronunciamento?Id=35)
- [CPC 27 — Property, Plant and Equipment](https://www.cpc.org.br/CPC/Documentos-Emitidos/Pronunciamentos/Pronunciamento?Id=58)
- [IAS 38 — Intangible Assets](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-38-intangible-assets/)
- [IFRS 13 — Fair Value Measurement](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-13-fair-value-measurement/)
