# ECO-1 — Decision Register

**Status:** Living public index · **Version:** 0.1 · **Date:** 2026-11-20

This register tracks topics for structured investigation. **All items are open** unless a later, reviewed revision records otherwise. Candidate directions are not adopted policies or operational claims.

| ID | Decision to be studied | Current status | Next analytical deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **D1** | Nature of evidence, represented assets, and legal/economic rights | Open; separation of functions under examination | Asset-class comparison with issuers, holders, rights, lifecycle, and regulatory questions |
| **D2** | Evidence thresholds, recognition authority, issuance, transfer, and retirement | Open | Asset-lifecycle and authorization framework |
| **D3** | Enterprise revenues, third-party payments, network incentives, and guarantees | Open | Economic-flow mapping and accounting boundaries |

## D1 — Detailed research questions

- D1.1 — What is a mere evidence record and what, if anything, is a legal claim?
- D1.2 — Which party can issue, recognize, own, transfer, or extinguish each proposed right?
- D1.3 — Does any network-native utility instrument offer a necessary function?
- D1.4 — How are distinct representations prevented from creating duplicate claims on the same underlying asset or outcome?
- D1.5 — What regulatory and contractual analysis applies to each concrete product?

**Materials to develop:** a class-by-class matrix and a comparison of competing historical formulations, with provenance kept in the appropriate disclosure environment.

## D2 — Detailed research questions

- D2.1 — What controls support recognition of each category of physical or operational event?
- D2.2 — Which decisions are automated, which require institutional authorization, and which require independent evidence?
- D2.3 — How can challenges, corrections, and invalidations affect already recognized rights?
- D2.4 — What constitutes completion, redemption, cancellation, or retirement for each category?
- D2.5 — How will field validation connect to the final authorization rules?

**Materials to develop:** a lifecycle diagram, evidence-to-rights responsibility map, and validation criteria.

## D3 — Detailed research questions

- D3.1 — Who pays for the underlying physical good or service?
- D3.2 — Who pays for verification infrastructure, and under what contractual arrangement?
- D3.3 — Which cash flows belong to third parties versus the PoWV technology provider?
- D3.4 — How should service fees, reserves, guarantees, and potential losses be treated economically?
- D3.5 — What model supports operating costs, CAPEX, OPEX, margins, and capital requirements?
- D3.6 — Under which conditions, if any, would shared validator incentives be economically justified?

**Materials to develop:** funds-flow map, enterprise-versus-operator model boundaries, and financing work plan.

## Update discipline

Each future decision should record: **status**, **date**, **scope**, **source/provenance**, **alternatives considered**, **dependencies**, and **approval authority**. Open questions should not be silently converted into adopted rules.

Public entries should summarize conclusions at an appropriate disclosure level. Nonpublic economic parameters and proprietary implementation details remain outside this repository.
