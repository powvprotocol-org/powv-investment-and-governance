# Corporate Structure

The founder currently operates as a Brazilian individual microentrepreneur (*microempreendedor individual*, MEI). The planned transition to a limited liability company is tied to the first commercial contract, pilot or equity investment.

| Milestone | Planned structure |
| --- | --- |
| First contract or pilot | If the founder remains the sole owner, the operating company would be a single-member *sociedade limitada* (SLU). It would sign the contract, invoice the customer and take responsibility for delivery. Microenterprise (ME) is a size classification available to an eligible company. |
| First equity investor | The MEI cannot admit a partner. The operating Ltda. would receive the investment and the investor would subscribe for quotas. The founder and investor could form the Ltda. together if the investment comes first. |
| Possible group structure | Following legal, accounting and tax review, an IP holding company could hold documented PoWV rights and license them to an operating subsidiary. The purpose would be to manage the IP separately from pilot delivery and customer obligations. |

Corporate ownership on either side of a holding–subsidiary structure generally precludes the preferential treatment under article 3(4) of Complementary Law 123/2006. The group’s tax assumptions would therefore need a fresh assessment. [Complementary Law 123/2006, art. 3](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp123.htm)
